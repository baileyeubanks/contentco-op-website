Source: the six blade paths extracted by Builder from apps/home/media/cco_logo/Main_LockUp.ai into aperture-mark-source.svg. Original coordinates, clipping, and viewBox (0 93 888 774) are preserved; the mark was not redrawn.

Colours: Sapphire #1e4d8c for light surfaces; cream #f3ebdf for dark surfaces; currentColor for inherited colour. SVGs include a title, role, and Content Co-op aria-label.

Transparent square PNG exports: 512, 192, 32, and 16 px in Sapphire and cream. The mark is centred, with about 8% horizontal padding and its original aspect ratio.

Aperture mark: at least 24px tall on any digital surface (the 16px and 32px favicon exports are the only smaller uses); at least 0.5" tall in print. Clear space = 1x the mark height on all sides.

Branding uses the aperture mark alone. Images use alt/aria-label "Content Co-op". The company name appears as text only in body copy and titles.
